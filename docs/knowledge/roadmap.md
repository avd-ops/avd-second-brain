# Knowledge base roadmap

This KB finished moving onto the family format on 2026-10-19, in five steps: the foundation,
the decisions in MADR 4, seed content from the connected repos' lessons, the experts study,
and the guardrails in the README and CLAUDE.md. The engine's data in
[`knowledge/`](../../knowledge/README.md) and the lessons in the connected repos stayed where
they were.

What came out of it is recorded in the decisions:
[0006](decisions/0006-family-kb-format.md) to
[0015](decisions/0015-family-format-github-work-conventions.md). How the KB is kept current
is in [curation.md](curation.md).

Open work (pages still to get upstream links, family proposals waiting on the other KBs) belongs
in the project's private log, not here
([decision 0024](decisions/0024-the-brain-is-the-estate-brain.md)).
